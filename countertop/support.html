Help with adding recipes and cooking with Countertop.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a recipe?

Tap + in the Recipes list and choose Paste Text, Web Link, Photos or Screenshots, or Scan Cookbook Page. Countertop shows every line with a label — Ingredient, Step, Note and so on. Check them, change any that are wrong, and tap Save.

Will Countertop change my recipe?

No. It only decides which lines are ingredients and which are steps. The words stay exactly as written. When you scale a recipe, only the numbers in the ingredient amounts change.

A line ended up in the wrong place.

Open the recipe, tap the ••• button and choose Fix Labels. Change the label next to the line and save.

How do timers work?

Cooking times written in a step, like “simmer 10–12 minutes”, are highlighted. Tap one to start a timer. The first time, iOS asks to allow alarms so the timer can ring on the Lock Screen and through Silent mode. For a range, the timer uses the shorter time so you can check early.

Why didn’t a web link work?

Some sites block apps from loading their pages, or put the recipe behind a sign-in. Try copying the recipe text from Safari and using Paste Text instead.

Which languages does it understand?

Recipes in English and Chinese. The app itself is in English.

Where are my recipes stored?

On your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes its recipes.
