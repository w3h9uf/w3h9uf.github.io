Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Data collection

Countertop does not collect personal data. It has no account system, advertising, tracking, or analytics SDK, and we operate no servers that receive your recipes or usage information.

Your recipes

Recipes you add are stored on your iPhone. The text you paste, the pages you link and the images you scan are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us or to any third party.

If you use iPhone backups, Apple may include Countertop’s data in your iCloud or computer backup. Those backups are controlled by you and governed by Apple’s terms.

Web links

When you add a recipe from a web link, your iPhone downloads that page directly from the website, the same way Safari would. The website may see your IP address as with any visit. Countertop sends nothing else and does not contact any other server.

On-device processing

Text in photos and scanned pages is recognized on your iPhone with Apple’s Vision framework. On devices with Apple Intelligence, Countertop may use Apple’s on-device language model to sort lines it cannot sort by itself. This runs on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us.

Device permissions

Countertop asks for the camera only when you scan a cookbook page, and for alarms when you first start a timer so it can ring when your phone is locked. Photos you pick are shared with the app only for that import; Countertop does not get access to your photo library. It does not request location, contacts, health data, the microphone, or the tracking identifier.

Deleting your data

Deleting a recipe removes it from the app. Deleting Countertop from your iPhone removes all recipes stored by the app.

Children

Countertop is not directed to children under 13. Because we do not collect personal data, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
