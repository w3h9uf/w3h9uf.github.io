Languages: English · 简体中文

Get started

Begin in planning mode. Buy a GPU and fans, arrange airflow, then tap 开始经营 to start time. Four real seconds equal one in-game hour. Open 浏览算力订单 to find contracts.

How do I move, repair or shut down hardware?

Tap a placed device to open its controls. Choose 调整位置 and tap the cell for its bottom-left corner. The same panel lets you repair, power off/on or sell a device. Fan controls include rotation.

Why is compute output falling?

Heat, hardware condition and power limits reduce deliverable compute. Check temperature, arrange directional fan cooling and keep within your facility’s power capacity. Pause planning before reorganizing.

Where is my progress?

Progress and a prior valid backup are stored on your device. Leaving the app pauses and saves; there are no offline earnings. There is no developer-operated cloud sync. Resetting the game clears current progress and its backup. Uninstalling may remove local progress.

How do I disable sound?

Tap 音效 开 to switch sound off. The preference is remembered on the device.

Contact support

Email info@hashingcorp.com with the app version, device model, iOS/iPadOS version and steps to reproduce the issue. A screenshot of the game can help.

算力霸权支持

从车库采购显卡与风扇，规划散热后点击「开始经营」。每4秒推进一个游戏小时；「浏览算力订单」可以查看客户需求。

设备操作

点设备打开面板，可移动、维护、开关机或出售。选择「调整位置」后，点目标格作为设备左下角。风扇可以旋转方向，冷却箭头前方三格。

存档与暂停

进度与上一份有效备份保存在设备本地，切到后台自动暂停并保存，没有离线收益。当前没有开发者提供的云同步。确认「重新开局」会清除本局和备份，卸载可能丢失进度。

声音与问题反馈

点「音效 开」即可关闭音效。如遇问题，请发送应用版本、设备型号、系统版本和复现步骤到 info@hashingcorp.com。
