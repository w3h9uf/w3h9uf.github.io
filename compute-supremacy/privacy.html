Privacy policy for Compute Supremacy / 算力霸权
Updated September 13, 2026 · Current offline version

Data used by the game

The current game does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking or third-party analytics. It does not send gameplay progress or device interaction data to a developer-operated server.

The app stores progress, hardware layout, temperatures, milestones, unread operation reports and sound preferences locally. It also keeps the previous valid save as a recovery backup. These records let you continue and recover your game.

Control and deletion

Confirming a new game clears current progress and its local backup. Sound preferences are stored separately. Uninstalling may delete local data. Operating-system backup services are controlled by your device settings and do not give the developer access to your save.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send and use it to respond and investigate your request. You may ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard website request information under its privacy statement. These support pages contain no advertising or third-party analytics scripts.

Contact and changes

Questions: info@hashingcorp.com. If the game later introduces accounts, network services or analytics, this policy and the store privacy declaration will be updated.

隐私说明

当前版本无需账号，不含广告、追踪或第三方统计，不向开发者服务器发送游戏进度或设备操作数据。

应用在设备本地保存经营进度、设备布局、温度、里程碑、未读报告和音效偏好，并保留上一份有效存档帮助恢复。确认重新开局会清除本局与备份，音效偏好独立保存。卸载可能清除本地数据。系统备份由设备设置控制，不代表开发者可以访问存档。

支持邮件与网站

如果你主动发送支持邮件，我们会收到邮箱地址及你提供的信息，用于回复与排查问题。你可以要求删除支持邮件，但适用的保留义务除外。本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，网站请求信息由 GitHub 按其隐私声明处理；页面没有广告或第三方统计脚本。

联系：info@hashingcorp.com。未来如增加联网、账号或分析功能，将同步更新本说明与商店隐私声明。
