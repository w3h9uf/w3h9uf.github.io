Support

Help with recording on Apple Watch and reading your day on iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start recording?

Open Dayscript on your Apple Watch and tap Start. The watch shows “Recording” and a running timer. Tap Stop to end.

Why did recording pause?

Phone calls, Siri, alarms, and other apps that use the microphone can interrupt recording. Dayscript tries to resume by itself; if it cannot, the watch sends a notification. Open Dayscript on the watch to continue.

When do recordings show up on my iPhone?

Recordings are sent in five-minute segments. Apple Watch decides when to transfer them, so they can arrive a few minutes late, and sooner when the iPhone is nearby and Dayscript has been opened recently.

Why does a segment say “Waiting to transcribe”?

Transcription runs on your iPhone, mostly while Dayscript is open. Open the app and leave it on screen to let it catch up. The first time you use a language, iOS needs an internet connection to download its speech model.

Which languages are supported?

English and Mandarin Chinese. Choose one in Settings. Speakers are not labeled separately.

Where is my data?

On your Apple Watch until it transfers, then on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes its recordings and transcripts.

Is it legal to record conversations?

It depends on where you are. Many places require everyone’s consent. Tell the people around you that you are recording.
